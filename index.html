Our Story

Awesome Kids Uganda was established by a team of dedicated persons who sacrifice a little from their earnings to respond to the challenges affecting children and families in underserved communities, including poverty, violence, abuse, neglect, child labor, school dropout, disability, family breakdown, and limited economic opportunities.

What We Do

Holistic programs designed to uplift and empower.

Education & Mentorship

Empowering children through quality education, scholastic materials, and life skills.

Health & Wellbeing

Promoting physical and mental health, hygiene, and nutrition for children and families.

Family Support

Strengthening vulnerable families through positive parenting and caregiver support.

Child Protection

Identification, prevention, and response to child abuse, neglect, and exploitation.

Empowerment

Equipping adolescents and caregivers with vocational skills and livelihood support.

Advocacy

Community awareness on children's rights and prevention of harmful practices.
